Questions and Answers
---------------------

1. Which variable has the highest VIF?
   Answer: numDeliveries (VIF = 17.3531).

2. Which variables show evidence of serious multicollinearity?
   Answer: milesTravelled (VIF = 14.94) and numDeliveries (VIF = 17.35)
   show serious multicollinearity since both exceed VIF = 10. gasPrice
   (VIF = 1.71) is low and not collinear.

3. In your own words, what does a high VIF mean?
   Answer: A high VIF means the variable is almost fully explained by the
   other predictors, so it carries little unique information of its own.
   The regression cannot isolate its separate effect on travelTime, making
   its coefficient unstable and its p-value unreliable.

4. Why might milesTravelled and numDeliveries be highly collinear?
   Answer: Because more deliveries naturally require more miles traveled,
   so the two move together and the data cannot separate their individual
   effects on travelTime.

5. Should the variable with the highest VIF automatically be removed?
   Explain.
   Answer: No, not automatically. Removal should be based on theory and the
   model's purpose. Here, dropping one of the collinear variables (e.g.,
   numDeliveries) is sensible for stable interpretation, but which variable
   to keep depends on substantive reasoning, not just the VIF value.
