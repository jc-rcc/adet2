Part 10 - Prediction
====================

Use the final model to predict travelTime for a delivery trip with:
milesTravelled = 90
numDeliveries = 4
gasPrice = 3.60

Predicted travelTime: 6.8021 hours

Interpretation:
For a delivery trip with 90 miles travelled and a gas price of $3.60,
the expected travel time is approximately 6.80 hours.

Note: The prediction uses the final (reduced) model from Part 8, which
uses milesTravelled and gasPrice. numDeliveries was removed in Part 8
due to multicollinearity, so it is not included in the prediction even
though its value was provided in the problem.
